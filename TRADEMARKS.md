## Allowed without asking

- Truthful nominative use that refers to the project, such as "based on Kaiflow" or "a fork of Kaiflow".

- Linking to the project, its repository or its website.
- Talks, articles, tutorials, reviews and courses about Kaiflow.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using the name or logo for a fork or a modified version. A fork must use a distinct name, logo and bundle
  identifier; it may say that it is "based on Kaiflow". The rebrand checklist in
  [docs/forking.md](docs/forking.md) lists every place to change.
- Names that are confusingly similar, such as "Kaiflow Pro" or "KaiFlow+".
- Using the name or logo in a company, product, service or domain name.
- Implying that CarlosLeonCode sponsors, endorses or supports your product, service or fork.
